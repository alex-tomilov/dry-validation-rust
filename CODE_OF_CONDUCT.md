# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in `dry-validation-rust` should be respectful, technically
honest, and welcoming. Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- give and receive specific, constructive feedback;
- respect differing experience levels and communication styles;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and the
  publication of another person's private information;
- stop behavior when asked by a maintainer.

Technical disagreement is normal. Persistent hostility, intimidation, or
personal targeting is not.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
threads, project events, and public spaces where someone is representing the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For project-specific concerns, contact the maintainer through the private
contact method available on the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/alex-tomilov). For threats, harassment, or
other platform abuse, use
[GitHub's private abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Do not post sensitive evidence publicly.

The maintainer may remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled
as privately as practical, subject to legal and safety obligations. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
